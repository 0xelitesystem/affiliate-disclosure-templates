# Video: on-screen text overlay

## Use when

You want a visual disclosure that's visible regardless of audio (helps viewers with sound off, accessibility, and people skipping around).

## Placement

- Top or bottom of the screen
- Persists for at least 5 seconds when first shown
- Re-appears at every visual transition involving the disclosed product
- Readable contrast against any background (white text + dark stroke, or solid color band)

## Text variants

### Affiliate

> Affiliate links in description

### Paid promotion / sponsored

> Includes paid promotion

### Sponsored segment

> Sponsored segment starts here

### Gifted product

> Product provided free by [BRAND]

### Sponsored review

> Sponsored — paid review

## Why this matters

- Many viewers watch with audio off (mobile, public transit)
- Some viewers skip into the middle of videos and miss top-of-video spoken disclosures
- A visual overlay during the sponsored segment is the most universally readable disclosure
