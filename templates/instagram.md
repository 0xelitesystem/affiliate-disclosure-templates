# Instagram disclosure

## Templates by post type

### Feed post (caption)

> **#ad #sponsored**
>
> [Your caption]
>
> Paid partnership with @brand.

Use Instagram's built-in "Paid partnership with @brand" tag (Settings -> Account -> Branded content). It surfaces a label above the post automatically. Your caption disclosure is in addition.

### Reel (caption + on-screen)

- On-screen text in the first 3 seconds: "Paid partnership with @brand" or "#ad"
- Caption opens with the disclosure (don't bury it after a hook)

### Story

- "Paid partnership" sticker (Instagram's built-in tool), AND
- Add a text overlay: "#ad" or "Paid partnership with @brand"
- For Stories with affiliate links via the link sticker: text overlay "Affiliate link"

### Gifted product

> Gifted by @brand — opinions are mine.

(Use this even when you weren't paid. The FTC considers free product a material connection.)

## What's NOT compliant

- `#sp`, `#partner`, `#collab`, `#thanks` (not specific enough)
- `#ad` buried in a wall of hashtags at the bottom of a long caption
- Disclosing only in your bio
- Disclosing only via Instagram's Paid Partnership tag (use it AND say it in caption)
