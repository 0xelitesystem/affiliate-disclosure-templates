# Video: spoken disclosure (sponsor segment)

## Use when

The video has a sponsored segment within otherwise independent content. The disclosure must come BEFORE the sponsored segment, not after.

## Templates

### Standard segment intro

> "Quick word from today's sponsor, [BRAND]. They asked me to mention [PRODUCT] because [reason]. Here's what they offer..."

### Tested-the-product version

> "I want to tell you about [BRAND] who sponsored today's video. I've actually been using [PRODUCT] for [time], so this isn't a script — here's what I think."

### Discount code version

> "Today's sponsor is [BRAND]. Use the link in the description and code [CODE] for [DISCOUNT]. I get a commission on sign-ups, which helps me make these videos."

## Standard segment outro

> "Okay, that's the end of the sponsored part. Back to the video."

## Why the outro matters

The audience needs to know when sponsored content ends so they don't think the rest of the video is also paid promotion. The outro is a signal of intellectual honesty, not just compliance.

## Notes

- Don't bury the sponsor segment between two parts of the same topic. Make it visually and verbally distinct.
- If you're using YouTube's "paid promotion" toggle, the visual indicator will show. That's fine, still do the spoken disclosure.
