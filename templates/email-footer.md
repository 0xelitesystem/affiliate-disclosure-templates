# Email / newsletter: footer disclosure

## Use as supplementary, not primary

Footer disclosures alone do not meet the FTC's "clear and conspicuous, before the recommendation" requirement. Use a footer disclosure to:

- Re-state the disclosure for thoroughness
- Provide unsubscribe + privacy links (legal requirement separate from disclosure)
- Link to a full sponsorship policy page

## Standard footer

```
---

You're receiving this because you subscribed at [URL].

Some links in this newsletter are affiliate links. We earn a commission on
purchases at no extra cost to you. We only recommend things we'd use ourselves.

Sponsorship inquiries: [email]
Unsubscribe: [link]
```

## CAN-SPAM (US) and similar laws

Email marketing has additional legal requirements beyond FTC endorsement disclosure:
- Physical mailing address (required)
- Clear unsubscribe link (required)
- Identification of the sender as commercial when applicable

These are separate from disclosure compliance. Don't conflate them.
