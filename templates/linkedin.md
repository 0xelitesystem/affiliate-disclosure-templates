# LinkedIn disclosure

LinkedIn's audience expects more formal language than other platforms. Disclose at the top of the post, in the first 1-2 lines (since LinkedIn truncates long posts).

## Templates

### Sponsored post

> **Disclosure: This is a sponsored post in partnership with [BRAND].**
>
> [your post content]

### Affiliate / referral link

> **Disclosure: Includes a referral link, I receive credit if you sign up.**
>
> [your post content]

### Advisor / equity

> **Disclosure: I'm an advisor to [COMPANY] mentioned in this post.**
>
> [your post content]

### Employee endorsement

> **Disclosure: I work at [COMPANY] and this post is about our product.**
>
> [your post content]

## Why disclose on LinkedIn

LinkedIn's professional context can make sponsored content feel like authentic recommendation when it isn't. The FTC specifically calls out professional networks as places where the line between organic and paid content is most easily blurred.
