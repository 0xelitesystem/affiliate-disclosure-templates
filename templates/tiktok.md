# TikTok disclosure

## On-screen + caption + verbal (use all three when possible)

### Sponsored video

- **On-screen** (first 3 seconds, in the safe area): "PAID PARTNERSHIP WITH [BRAND]"
- **Caption**: opens with `#ad` or "Sponsored by @brand"
- **Verbal**: in the first 3 seconds: "This video is sponsored by [BRAND]"
- **TikTok's tool**: enable "Promote brand" / "Branded content" toggle when uploading

### Affiliate links / shop links

- On-screen: "Affiliate link below" or "I earn commission"
- Caption: `#ad #affiliate Link in bio` (then "link in bio" must lead to a clear page where the affiliate nature is also disclosed)

### Gifted product

- On-screen: "GIFTED BY [BRAND]"
- Caption: "[BRAND] sent me this, opinions are mine. #gifted"

## Why TikTok needs all three

TikTok's auto-play and high-skip behavior means viewers may miss any single channel. Belt-and-suspenders disclosure is the only way to reliably reach viewers regardless of how they consume the video.

## Don't

- Don't rely on `#ad` as a hashtag at the END of a long hashtag list
- Don't put the disclosure in a comment instead of the caption
- Don't disclose only in the duet/stitch context if the original was independent
