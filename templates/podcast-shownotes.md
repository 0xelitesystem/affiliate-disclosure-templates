# Podcast: show notes disclosure

## Standard show notes structure

```
EPISODE [#]: [TITLE]

[Brief episode description]

This episode is brought to you by:
- [SPONSOR 1], [URL with affiliate code if applicable]
- [SPONSOR 2], [URL]

Affiliate disclosure: links to products mentioned in this episode may be affiliate links. The podcast earns a commission on purchases at no extra cost to you.

LINKS MENTIONED:
- [Topic / product]: [URL]
- [Topic / product]: [URL]

TIMESTAMPS:
00:00 - Intro
03:21 - First sponsor
05:12 - Today's topic
...
```

## Why show notes need their own disclosure

- Many listeners look at show notes to find products mentioned
- The audio disclosure may not have explicitly named which links in the show notes are affiliate
- Show notes are often read on a different device/session than the podcast was heard on

## Don't

- Don't list sponsors as if they're part of the episode topic ("In this episode we discuss [Product] from [Sponsor]") without separating them
- Don't omit affiliate disclosure from show notes just because you said it in audio
