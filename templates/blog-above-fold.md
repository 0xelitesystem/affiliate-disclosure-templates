# Blog: above-the-fold disclosure

## Use when

The blog post contains affiliate links, sponsored content, or recommendations of products you have a material connection with. Place the disclosure above the first such recommendation, ideally in the first paragraph or as a separate notice block right at the top.

## Template

### Affiliate-only

> **Disclosure:** This post contains affiliate links. If you buy through them, I earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. I only recommend products I've actually used or thoroughly researched.

### Sponsored post

> **Disclosure:** This post is sponsored by [BRAND]. They paid me to write about [TOPIC]. The opinions, recommendations, and any criticism are my own.

### Gifted product review

> **Disclosure:** [BRAND] sent me [PRODUCT] for free for this review. They did not pay for the review and do not have any approval over the content.

### Mixed sources

> **Disclosure:** This post mentions products I've received as samples, products I've bought myself, and one sponsored placement (called out where it appears). Affiliate links are noted where present.

## Format notes

- Use a visually distinct format (bold heading "Disclosure", a notice box, or a horizontal rule above and below)
- Don't bury inside a long intro paragraph
- Don't link to a separate disclosure page as a substitute
- Specifying which links are affiliate (e.g. "*affiliate" after each link) is good practice but not required if the top-of-post disclosure covers the post
