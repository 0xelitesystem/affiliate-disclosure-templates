# Video: spoken disclosure (top of video)

## Use when

You are recommending or showcasing a product/service in the video and have one or more of: affiliate links, sponsorship, gifted product, or other material connection. Spoken at the top of the video before any recommendation.

## Templates

### Affiliate links only

> "Quick heads-up: some of the links in the description are affiliate links, which means I earn a small commission if you buy something through them at no extra cost to you. That helps support the channel."

### Sponsored video (full sponsorship)

> "This video is sponsored by [BRAND]. They paid for this video, but the opinions and demo you'll see are my own. More on that in a minute."

### Partial sponsorship (sponsored segment)

> "Before we get into it: today's video has a sponsored section from [BRAND]. I'll let you know exactly when that starts. The rest of the video is independent."

### Gifted product (no payment, no posting requirement)

> "Heads-up: [BRAND] sent me this product for free, but they didn't pay me to make this video and they don't get to see it before it goes live."

### Free sample for honest review

> "[BRAND] sent me this for review. I wasn't paid, and they don't have approval over what I say. Here's what I actually think."

### Equity / advisor relationship

> "Disclosure: I'm an advisor to / I own equity in [COMPANY]. Take that into account as I describe what they do."

## Why this works

- Clear (no jargon, no hashtag shorthand)
- Conspicuous (spoken at the top, before the recommendation)
- Specific (names the relationship type)
- Unavoidable (you can't skip past it without missing the start of the video)

## Notes

- Pair the spoken disclosure with text in the description and (where applicable) on-screen text. Belt and suspenders.
- For sponsored videos, follow the platform's tools too (YouTube has a "paid promotion" toggle that surfaces a notification). The platform tool is in addition to your disclosure, not a replacement.
