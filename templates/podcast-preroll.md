# Podcast: pre-roll spoken disclosure

## Use when

Beginning of every episode that contains sponsored segments, affiliate links in show notes, or any recommendation with a material connection.

## Templates

### Single sponsor

> "Today's episode is brought to you by [BRAND]. We'll have more on them later in the show. Let's get into it."

### Multiple sponsors

> "Today's episode is brought to you by [BRAND 1] and [BRAND 2]. You'll hear from both of them in dedicated segments later. Now to the episode."

### No sponsors but affiliate links in show notes

> "Quick note: the show notes for this episode include affiliate links. If you click and buy, the show earns a small commission at no extra cost to you. Helps keep the show going."

### Episode with a sponsored guest

> "Heads-up: today's guest, [NAME], is from [COMPANY], who is sponsoring this episode. The conversation is genuine but they did pay for the slot."

## Why pre-roll matters

Podcast listeners often start an episode and let it play. A disclosure at minute 23 reaches fewer ears than one at minute 0. Pre-roll is the only spot guaranteed to reach everyone who listens to ANY of the episode.
