# Podcast: mid-roll spoken disclosure

## Use when

A specific sponsored segment in the middle of the episode. The mid-roll disclosure is in addition to the pre-roll, not a replacement.

## Templates

### Standard mid-roll

> "We'll be right back after a word from today's sponsor, [BRAND]."
>
> [Read the sponsor copy]
>
> "And we're back. Where were we?"

### Host-read with personal experience

> "Quick break for our sponsor [BRAND]. I want to tell you about [PRODUCT] because I actually use it for [purpose]. [Personal pitch.] [BRAND], thanks for sponsoring."

### Discount code mid-roll

> "Today's sponsor is [BRAND]. Use code [CODE] at [URL] for [DISCOUNT]. We get a commission on sign-ups, which we appreciate."

## What to AVOID in mid-roll

- Treating the sponsored segment as if it's part of the regular conversation without a clear "we're going to a sponsor break"
- Putting the sponsored segment in a way that's edited to feel like a guest interview when it's a paid placement
- Skipping the disclosure because "the audience knows"
