# Blog: end-of-post disclosure

## Use when

NEVER as the only disclosure. End-of-post disclosure is supplementary; the FTC requires disclosure BEFORE or alongside the recommendation, not after.

End-of-post is fine for:

- Restating the disclosure for thoroughness
- Adding context (how you got the product, why you reviewed it, when you bought it)
- Linking to your full disclosure policy

## Template

```
---

**About this post:** [Repeat the relationship: affiliate, sponsored, gifted, etc.] I bought the [PRODUCT] in [MONTH YEAR] / received the [PRODUCT] as a sample on [DATE]. I have/have not been compensated by [BRAND] beyond what's described above.

For my full affiliate and sponsorship policy, see [/disclosures](https://yoursite.com/disclosures).
```

## Note

The "for my full policy" link is supplementary, never a replacement for in-content disclosure. The FTC has been explicit: a separate policy page does not satisfy the requirement to disclose at the point of recommendation.
